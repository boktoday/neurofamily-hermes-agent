# Crisis card

_One printable page that tells any adult what they need to know, fast. Fill in once; review monthly; keep a copy on the fridge, in school bags, and on phones._

---

## [Child's name]

- Age: [age]
- Diagnoses (one line, plain words): [e.g. autistic, ADHD, ARFID; non-speaking]
- Speaks / understands: [how to communicate with them, and how to check they understood]

## If they are overwhelmed, melting down or shut down

- **What helps:** [their reliable moves: quiet room, deep pressure, dark, headphones, a specific person]
- **What to avoid:** [touch, eye contact, questions, crowds, raised voices]
- **How long it usually takes:** [minutes, hours]
- **Afterwards they need:** [rest, water, quiet, time before talking]

## Medical

- Medications: [name, dose, when]
- Allergies: [list; reactions]
- Conditions responders should know: [e.g. epilepsy, diabetes]
- Food: [safe foods available; never force food]

## Key contacts (in order)

1. [Parent/carer name, phone]
2. [Second contact]
3. [GP / paediatrician]
4. [Other]

## If I need help right now

- Emergency: 000 (AU) / 999 (UK) / 911 (US)
- [Regional crisis line from `shared/regional/`]
- [Local hospital or urgent care]

## Made with [child]'s input on [date]

- [Anything they want responders to know in their own words. Add it exactly as they say it.]

---

_This card is a family document. It is not a legal or medical record, and it does not replace the plans held by school or health services._
