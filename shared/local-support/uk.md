# Local support: United Kingdom

Last verified: October 2026. The agent re-checks every link when the list is built, and again each quarter.

## Crisis, right now

- **Emergency: 999** (police, ambulance, fire). If danger is immediate, this first, and a trusted adult in the room.
- **NHS 111, option 2** — the 24/7 urgent mental health line in England; in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland use NHS 111 or your local urgent service.
- **Samaritans: 116 123** (24/7, free).
- **Shout: text 85258** (24/7 crisis text line).
- **Childline: 0800 1111** (free, for anyone under 19).

## Autism

- **National Autistic Society helpline: 0808 800 4104** — Monday to Thursday 10am to 4pm, Friday 9am to 3pm. Impartial, confidential information and advice. **Not a crisis service.**
- National Autistic Society also runs online community and urgent-help signposting: autism.org.uk

## ADHD

- **ADHD UK** — information, advocacy and community: adhduk.co.uk
- **ADHD Adults UK** — peer community for adults: adhdadults.uk
- **ADDISS** — information and resources: addiss.co.uk
- The NHS route: GP first; for children, also the school's SENCO.

## Broader support and systems

- **Mind Infoline: 0300 123 3393** — information and signposting, 9am to 6pm weekdays.
- **SANEline: 0300 304 7000** — 4.30pm to 10.30pm, every day.
- **Carers UK** — support for carers: carersuk.org
- **Your GP, health visitor (under 5s) and the SENCO at school** — the first doors for assessment pathways.

## Country note

The UK's four nations run different health and education systems. The agent should confirm which nation the family is in and adjust referrals accordingly (for example, Education, Health and Care plans exist in England).
