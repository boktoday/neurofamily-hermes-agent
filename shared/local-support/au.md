# Local support: Australia

Last verified: October 2026. The agent re-checks every link when the list is built, and again each quarter. Numbers and hours do change; trust the live check over this file.

## Crisis, right now

- **Emergency: 000** (police, ambulance, fire). If danger is immediate, this first, and a trusted adult in the room.
- **Lifeline: 13 11 14** (24/7, any crisis, any age). Text service: 0477 13 11 14.
- **Kids Helpline: 1800 55 1800** (24/7, ages 5 to 25).
- **13YARN: 13 92 76** (24/7, Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander crisis support).
- **Parentline: 13 22 89** (free parenting support; hours vary by state).
- **Suicide Call Back Service: 1300 659 467** (24/7, for anyone affected by suicide).
- **MensLine Australia: 1300 78 99 78** (24/7).
- **Beyond Blue: 1300 22 4636** (24/7 mental health support).

## Autism

- **Autism Connect (by Amaze): 1300 308 699** — the national autism helpline. 8am to 7pm, Monday to Friday. Phone, email or live webchat. Free, independent, and expert: diagnosis, school, NDIS, behaviour and communication, referrals, peer connections, autism-friendly places. **Not a crisis service**: in a crisis use the lines above.
- **Aspect** — services, education and employment programs: autismspectrum.org.au
- **Positive Partnerships** — free workshops and resources for families and schools: positivepartnerships.com.au

## ADHD

- **ADHD Foundation Australia national helpline: 1300 39 39 19** — pathway guidance, screening and referral questions. Volunteer-run charity, not 24 hours; leave a message or use the contact form and they call back.
- **ADHD Australia** — evidence-based information, advocacy and community: adhdaustralia.org.au
- **ADDults with ADHD: (02) 9889 5977** — free information and support groups: adultadhd.org.au
- State bodies also exist (for example ADHD WA: (08) 6457 7544).

## Broader support and systems

- **NDIS: 1800 800 110** — plus the two doors that matter: an **early childhood partner** (children under 7) or a **local area coordinator** (older children and adults). The agent can help find the right one for the family's area.
- **Carers Australia: 1800 242 636** — support for the parent doing the caring.
- **Headspace: 1800 650 890** — mental health for ages 12 to 25, 150+ centres.
- **Raising Children Network** — practical, evidence-based parenting information: raisingchildren.net.au
- **Your GP, paediatrician or child and family health nurse** — the honest first door for diagnosis pathways and referrals.

## Language help

- **TIS National: 131 450** — free interpreters. Call TIS first, then ask to be connected to any number above (for example: "Autism Connect on 1300 308 699").
