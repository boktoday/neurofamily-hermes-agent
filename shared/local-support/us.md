# Local support: United States

Last verified: October 2026. The agent re-checks every link when the list is built, and again each quarter.

## Crisis, right now

- **Emergency: 911** (police, ambulance, fire). If danger is immediate, this first, and a trusted adult in the room.
- **988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988** (24/7). Chat also available at 988lifeline.org.
- **Crisis Text Line: text HOME to 741741** (24/7).

## Autism

- **Autism Society of America** — information, referral and local affiliates: autism-society.org
- **Autistic Self Advocacy Network** — by and for autistic people: autisticadvocacy.org
- Local routes that matter most: the child's **paediatrician**, the **school district's special education office** (IEP process), and **Early Intervention** for children under 3 (IDEA Part C, run by each state).

## ADHD

- **CHADD** — information, parent training and local chapters: chadd.org
- **ADDitude** — practical articles and guides: additudemag.com
- The route to assessment: a paediatrician, family doctor or child psychiatrist; schools can also evaluate.

## Broader support

- **National Parent Helpline: 1-855-427-2736** — emotional support for parents.
- **Federation of Families / state family-run organisations** — parent-to-parent support, state by state.
- Health insurance questions: your insurer's member services line (the American system is the system; the agent should help navigate it, not guess at coverage).

## Country note

Services in the US are state and county based. The agent should confirm the state, then look for the state's early intervention program, parent training and protection agency contacts before presenting the list.
