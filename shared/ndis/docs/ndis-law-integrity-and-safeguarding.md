# NDIS laws: Integrity and safeguarding (2026)

> Source: https://www.ndis.gov.au/ndis-laws/integrity-and-safeguarding  
> Fetched 15 October 2026 by the NeuroFamily agent project. © National Disability Insurance Agency (NDIA), or the Commonwealth of Australia, as marked at the source. Text reproduced for noncommercial family support use only. Always verify current rules at ndis.gov.au.

The National Disability Insurance Scheme Amendment (Integrity and Safeguarding) Act 2026 was passed and achieved Royal Assent on 8 April 2026.

This law:

expands the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission’s powers to detect, prevent and respond to breaches of obligations under the Act
provides participants wishing to leave the NDIS with additional safeguards and communication options
enables the NDIA to move to an entirely electronic claiming system for providers
makes sure plan variations can include an increase or decrease of the total funding amounts.
