Introduction
The National Disability Insurance Agency (NDIA) is established under the National
Disability Insurance Scheme Act 2013 (the Act).
The National Disability Insurance Scheme Amendment (Securing the NDIS for
Future Generations) Act 2026 (the Amending Act) establishes a new framework for
determining pricing arrangements. Under these arrangements, the Minister for
Disability and the National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS) may make a pricing
determination by legislative instrument, including determining the maximum amount,
or the method for determining the maximum amount, for the acquisition or provision
of supports. The Amending Act also gives the NDIA a statutory function to provide
advice to the Minister on the pricing of supports.
This is the National Disability Insurance Scheme Pricing Schedule 2026-27. This
schedule is incorporated into the pricing determination in accordance with
subsections 45C(11) and 45C(12) of the Act. Subsection 45C(11) allows for the
incorporation of the documents as in force from time to time, while subsection 45(12)
provides for the incorporation of documents specially prepared for the purposes of
incorporation. The schedule lists the maximum amounts payable for NDIS supports
by the NDIA covered by this Schedule.
Maximum amounts payable for Specialist Disability Accommodation are set out in
the NDIS Pricing Schedule for Specialist Disability Accommodation 2026-27.
This document should be read in conjunction with the Pricing Determination.

Part 1: Maximum amounts
1.
General
1.1. Maximum amount for an NDIS support
a. In accordance with subsection 45C(2) of the National Disability Insurance
Scheme Act 2013 (the Act), the maximum amounts specified in Part 2
apply to the acquisition or provision of an NDIS support or class of NDIS
supports if the funding for the support or class of supports under a
participant’s plan is managed by:
i. The National Disability Insurance Agency (NDIA) (Agency-
managed); or
ii. A registered plan management provider (plan managed).
1.2. Inclusion of GST
a. Any maximum amount specified in this document already includes any
Goods and Services Tax (GST).
1.3.
Unit of Measure
a. Claims are made in line with the relevant Unit of Measure (Unit) listed in
Part 2. The maximum amounts apply per unit of measure. Unit of Measure
is defined in Part 4.
2.
Differentiated amounts
2.1. This section explains the pricing structure that applies to the maximum amounts
set out in Part 2. Subsection 45C(9) of the Act allows for different maximum
amounts to apply so that the amounts can account for factors like complexity
and intensity, geography, mode of delivery, and any other matters or
circumstances.
2.2. Complexity and intensity
a. The maximum amounts set out in Part 2 differ based on the complexity and
intensity of the support.
b. A provider may claim up to the maximum amount for a support item in Part
2 that references intensity and complexity in accordance with the definitions
in Part 4.
2.3. Geographic location
a. The maximum amounts set out in Part 2 differ based on the geographic
location where the support is delivered. This is determined by the physical
location where the service takes place, regardless of the participants’ usual
home address.
b. Geographic location is determined by reference to:

the Modified Monash Model (MMM) classification of the location
as defined in Part 4 – Definitions.
ii.

c. If a support is delivered in a MM 1-5 location, except for areas reclassified
under Part 3 – Isolated Towns Modification - Type 1, the national amount
applies.
d. If a support is delivered in an MM 6 (Remote) area or an area reclassified
as remote under Part 3 – Isolated Towns Modification - Type 1 as remote,
the remote amount applies.
e. If a support is delivered in a MM 7 (Very Remote) area or an area
reclassified as very remote under Part 3 – Isolated Towns Modification -
Type 1, the Very Remote amount applies.
f.
Where remote or very remote amounts are not listed for a support, that
support is not eligible for remote or very remote loading.
g. The MMM classification of some locations has been adjusted to allow for
additional adjustments to maximum amounts.
h. Part 3 contains the Isolated Towns Modification - Type 1 list. The NDIA
have reclassified the locations in this list as MM 6 (Remote) or MM 7 (Very
Remote) areas.
2.4. Day and time
a. The maximum amounts set out in Part 2 differ based on the day of week or
time of day at which the support is delivered.
b. A provider may claim up to the maximum amount for a support item in
Appendices 1, 2 and 3 that references the day of the week or the time of
the day in accordance with the definitions in Part 4.
c. Where a support a support crosses between different times of day or days
of week and is delivered by a single worker, the higher of the amounts
applies to the whole support.
2.5. Group-based supports
a. The maximum amounts set out in Part 2 differ based on whether the
support is delivered to more than one person at the same time (a group of
people).
b. The amount a provider may claim from each participant is calculated by
dividing the applicable maximum amount set out in the relevant schedule
by the number of people in the group.

a.

Unit
The following units define the structural billing increments for
claims, differentiating between time-based support delivery
and unit-based items:
• Hour: used to apply up to the hourly maximum amount,
where multiple hours can be billed in a single claim and
the amount is calculated as a full hour or a fraction of
the limit depending on the exact minutes the service
was active.
• Day: used to apply up to the daily maximum amount for
a single calendar date, where multiple days can be
billed in a single claim and the amount is calculated as a
full calendar day or a fraction of that day depending on
the exact hours or portion of the date the service was
active.
• Month: used to apply up to the monthly maximum
amount, where multiple months can be billed in a single
claim and the amount is calculated as a full calendar
month or a fraction of that month depending on the
exact days the service was active.
• Year: used to apply up to the annual maximum amount,
where multiple years can be billed in a single claim and
the amount is calculated as a full year or a fraction of
that year depending on the exact months or days the
service was active.
• Each: used to apply a maximum amount for unit or
instance, where multiple items or tasks can be billed in
a single claim and the amount is calculated strictly per
whole unit based on the exact quantity provided, as
these distinct deliverables are not billed in fractions.
