# Roadmap

Where the Hermes edition is going. Items move when families need them, not on a schedule.

## Shipped in v2.0.0 (October 2026)

- All twelve agents ported from the OpenClaw edition to Hermes skills.
- Shared-context templates created (the OpenClaw edition described the shared brain but shipped no files for it).
- Schedules added: daily routine anchors, weekly family check-in, carer wellbeing check-in, sibling check-in, monthly review.
- LICENSE added (PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0). The OpenClaw edition had none, which meant families had no clear rights at all.
- Local models guide added to INSTALL.md (promised in the old changelog, never shipped).
- Regional support refactored into `shared/regional/` with the missing `_template.md` added.
- Language standardised on "young people" throughout.

## Next

- **More regions.** Canada, New Zealand and a general EU framework, plus translation-ready regional templates so communities can localise.
- **Artifact pack (started in v2.2.0).** Food diary shipped as a local page plus a Sheets variant. Next: first-then board, feelings ladder, morning routine card, weekly family planner, all printable and personalisable.
- **Voice-first conversations.** Family-friendly voice notes in and out over Telegram, tuned for parents who are hands-full.
- **School meeting toolkit.** A guided prep flow for IEP/EHCP/504/ILP meetings: evidence gathering, agenda drafting, follow-up letter templates, region-aware.
- **Artifact gallery.** Example outputs from the Artifacts Generator so families can see what to ask for.
- **Evaluation notes.** A light, consented feedback loop so families can tell us what helped; aggregate only, never individual data.
- **Community contributions guide.** How to propose a regional resource, a schedule or a skill improvement via pull request.

## Ideas parked for now

- WhatsApp channel depth (depends on Hermes channel support).
- A moderated peer community for families (deliberately not rushed; moderation is the hard part, not the software).
- Translations of the full skill set (the language rules live in SOUL.md).
