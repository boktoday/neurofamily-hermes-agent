# Carer wellbeing check-in

**Skill:** parent-companion
**Default:** Wednesday 20:00.
**Purpose:** the parents matter too. A midweek moment for the adult, not the child.

## The prompt

> Load the parent-companion skill. Wedneday evening check-in for the parent or carer. Read family-profile.md for their current capacity and preferences. Write a short message (under 120 words): check in on THEM (their week, their energy), not the child's progress. Ask one gentle question. If capacity has been low for two or more weeks, offer one concrete respite idea sized to what they can actually arrange this week. No advice unless asked. Log notable things to shared-notes.md with care.

## Notes

- Rotate to whichever adult is in the profile if two parents use the assistant; or address the family together if they share one chat.
- If they answer with something heavy, the emotional coach or professional referral paths apply; parent burnout is a family issue.
