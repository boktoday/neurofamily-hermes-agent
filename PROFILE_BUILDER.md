# Family Profile Builder

**An interactive onboarding that creates your family's shared support picture.**

---

## What this is

The Profile Builder is a guided conversation with the Family Navigator. Instead of filling in a long form, families answer questions step by step and the Navigator writes the result into `~/neurofamily/family-profile.md` and `~/neurofamily/child-summary.md`.

Answer as much or as little as you like. Everything can be updated later, and nothing is shared anywhere: it all lives on your own machine.

---

## How to start

Tell the Navigator:

> "I want to set up our family profile."

It will lead from there. The conversation can be split across several sittings; the Navigator saves progress and resumes where you left off.

---

## What it covers (about 18 minutes total)

| Step | Topic | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | About the parents and carers | 3 min |
| 2 | About the children | 5 min |
| 3 | Diagnoses, differences and needs | 5 min |
| 4 | Daily life and routines | 3 min |
| 5 | What you need help with first | 2 min |

---

## The questions, in outline

**Step 1 - the adults.** Names and preferred names, who is in the household, timezone and location (so regional resources load correctly), languages spoken at home, communication preferences, and how much capacity you have right now (this changes week to week and the team adapts to it).

**Step 2 - the children.** For each child: name and age, what they are brilliant at, what is hard, how they communicate (words, AAC, scripts, signs), and anything about school or care arrangements.

**Step 3 - differences and needs.** Any diagnoses or differences (formal or suspected; suspected is fine), what supports are already in place, which professionals are involved, and what has helped or not helped before.

**Step 4 - daily life.** The shape of a normal day: mornings, school runs, after school, evenings, food, sleep. What works already. Where the friction is.

**Step 5 - priorities.** The top three things the family wants help with right now, in their own words. This drives which specialists the Navigator brings in first.

---

## Updating the profile

Say "let's update our family profile" and the Navigator will walk through just the parts that changed. The old versions are kept in the file so you can see how life has evolved.
